2. Title and story

Campaign title
Help Keep The Puppets Dancing: 10 Shows For Delaware’s Children
Story
Hello friends! It’s Ms. Jeanette Waters, CEO of Let God Help Resources! For over 40 years, my two hundred beloved puppets and I have been teaching Delaware’s children about kindness, culture, faith, and the joy of learning. From Christmas and Thanksgiving to Halloween and history, we have shared stories that children never forget. You may have seen our times tables puppets! Yes, we make math fun! We sing, we dance, we learn our ABCs and our times tables, because learning should be filled with love and laughter. This holiday season, I am not doing a big tour. Just ten special shows, right here in Delaware, plus online for our friends far away. Ten Puppets for Understanding shows, close to home, with time to hug every child after. But I need your help to preserve what we built. I have over 200 puppets, a beautiful stage, and decades of original scripts. What we need now is help to write new Cultural Literacy teacher guides and put them on flash drives for our daycares and churches. Our goal is $25,000. 100% of proceeds support Let God Help Resources, Inc. All photos are from my own collection. Will you help keep the puppets dancing? God bless you!
